About

Who we are

We are a community of builders, thinkers, and problem-solvers at Bangladesh Army International University of Science & Technology. BAIUST Computer Club exists to bridge the gap between academic curriculum and real-world engineering practice.

Through workshops, hackathons, mentorship, and open-source collaboration, we create an environment where technical skills flourish and lasting connections form.
