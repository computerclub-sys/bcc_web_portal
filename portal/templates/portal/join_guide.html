Join BAIUST Computer Club

Follow these simple steps to become a member.

1

Get Your QR Code

Visit any club executive or the club room to receive your unique membership QR code. This code is your key to accessing the portal.

2

Scan or Open the QR Link

Scan your QR code using any smartphone camera, or click the link sent to you. You'll be taken to the registration page with your code pre-filled.

3

Create Your Account

Fill in your name, email, and a password to create your portal account. Make sure to use a valid email — you'll use it to sign in later.

4

Complete Your Profile

After registering, add your phone number, department, and batch to complete your profile. This info helps the club stay connected with you.

5

You're In!

Sign in anytime to access your membership card, register for events, and track your payments. Welcome to the community!
